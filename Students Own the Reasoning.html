The assignment students receive

In this project students study questions they care about. They choose the story. GenAI builds the data. They do the statistics. They work in groups.

StageWhat students doGenAI
1. StoryChoose a story or area of interest and describe it in 2 to 4 sentences. Write at least 4 questions. Create at least 2 numeric variables, 2 qualitative variables, and 1 binary variable. Choose variables that can be compared between groups.Their own work
2. DatasetFill in the prompt and paste it into any GenAI tool. Save the completed prompt and the Excel file GenAI returns. Critique the dataset. Is it plausible? What did GenAI get wrong? Which assumptions do they disagree with?With GenAI
3. AnalysisOpen the Excel file and copy the data into Statdisk. State two hypotheses, one about one population and one comparing two populations. Test both.Without GenAI
4. MasteryWrite a report that shows they understand the statistics. Give an oral presentation to the class.Their own work

All data is invented. Students do not use real personal data.

The student prompt for statistics

Students fill in the brackets, then paste the whole prompt into any GenAI tool. They need at least four questions, two numeric variables, two qualitative variables, and one binary variable.

I am a statistics student. I need a realistic, made-up dataset for a class project. I will do all of the analysis myself.

MY STORY OR AREA OF INTEREST
[Describe your story or interest in 2 to 4 sentences.]

MY QUESTIONS (at least 4)
1. [Question]
2. [Question]
3. [Question]
4. [Question]
[Add more if you have them.]

MY VARIABLES
Numeric (at least 2)
- [Variable name] : [units]
- [Variable name] : [units]
Qualitative (at least 2)
- [Variable name] : [list the categories]
- [Variable name] : [list the categories]
Binary (at least 1)
- [Variable name] : [the two values, for example yes / no]
[Add more lines to any group if needed.]

SAMPLE SIZE
[Number of rows, for example 40]

YOUR TASK
1. Create a dataset that fits my story, with one row per individual and one column for each variable I listed. Do not add or remove variables.
2. Make the data usable for comparisons. Put enough rows in each value of the binary variable and each category of the qualitative variables that I can compare the numeric variables between groups.
3. Make the values realistic. Include natural variation. Do not make the data too clean.
4. Do not build in a result. Differences between groups may be large, small, or absent.
5. All data must be invented. Do not use real people or real records.
6. Give me the data as an Excel file (.xlsx) that I can download, with one sheet and a header row. If you cannot create a file, give me the data as a table that I can copy and paste into Excel.
7. After the data, list the assumptions you made to create it, in 3 to 5 short bullet points.
8. I may come back later in the course and ask for more rows or new variables for this same story. When I do, keep the same variable names, units, categories, and format so the new data fits with this dataset.

DO NOT
- Do not calculate any statistics.
- Do not run any tests.
- Do not interpret the data or tell me what it shows.

The prompt for any discipline

The pattern has three moves.

  1. Students author the context. They choose the topic, write the questions, and decide what the material must include.
  2. GenAI makes the raw material. A dataset, a case, a set of documents. Everything is invented.
  3. Students do the reasoning without GenAI. They analyze, judge, and explain.

You fill in the blanks in CAPITALS once for each assignment. Students fill in the rest.

I am a student in [COURSE OR DISCIPLINE]. I need realistic, made-up material for a class project. I will do all of the analysis and reasoning myself.

MY TOPIC OR STORY
[Describe your topic, story, or area of interest in 2 to 4 sentences.]

MY QUESTIONS (at least [NUMBER])
1. [Question you want to explore]
2. [Question]
3. [Question]
[Add more if you have them.]

WHAT THE MATERIAL MUST INCLUDE
- [Element you chose, and any details about it]
- [Element]
- [Element]
[Add more lines if needed.]

YOUR TASK
1. Create [TYPE OF MATERIAL] that fits my topic and includes every element I listed. Do not add or remove elements.
2. Make it realistic. Include the kind of detail, variation, and imperfection that real material of this type would have.
3. Do not build in an answer. The material may support more than one conclusion.
4. Everything must be invented. Do not use real people, real organizations, or real records.
5. Give me the material in this format: [FORMAT].
6. After the material, list the assumptions you made to create it, in 3 to 5 short bullet points.
7. I may come back later and ask for more material on this same topic. When I do, keep it consistent with what you created here.

DO NOT
- Do not [THE REASONING STUDENTS MUST DO THEMSELVES].
- Do not interpret the material or tell me what it shows.
- Do not answer my questions.
